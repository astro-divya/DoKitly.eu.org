What is checked?

DoKitly checks public website and domain signals. Full engine mode can inspect the target page server-side, including HTTP status and redirects, title/meta/H1/canonical, robots and sitemap availability, DNS, RDAP registration data, security headers, technology signals, optional public popularity signals and PageSpeed/Lighthouse when available. Traffic, ad-revenue and site-value figures are shown as broad labelled estimates from public signals; they are not private owner analytics and can differ materially from real data.
