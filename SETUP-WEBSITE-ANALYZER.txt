Build 12 analyzer changes
- Root/registrable domain is used for RDAP, NS, MX, TXT and popularity signals.
- The actual requested host remains in use for page fetch and A/AAAA checks.
- robots.txt discovery parses Sitemap: entries before fallback sitemap paths.
- Optional checks are isolated so one unavailable source does not erase the full report.
- CORS allows https://dokitly.in and https://www.dokitly.in.
- Traffic/value figures remain clearly labelled directional estimates rather than owner analytics.
