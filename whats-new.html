What’s New

See the latest useful additions and fixes in DoKitly.

Build 7 highlights

  • Website Analyzer live engine: domain-specific DNS, SEO, redirects, security and PageSpeed signals with the included Cloudflare Worker.
  • Typing Practice: English/Hindi typing test plus a Hindi typing tutor with lessons and layout guidance.
  • Custom home shortcuts: choose and reorder your own quick tools; preferences stay in your browser.
  • QR Studio: generate QR codes and decode QR images.
  • Cleaner navigation: Home, Categories and What’s New in the header, with support links moved to the footer.
  • All Tools navigation: sticky, scrollable category bar with active-section highlighting.

Still improving
