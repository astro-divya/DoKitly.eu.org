What’s New

See the latest useful additions and fixes in DoKitly.

Build 10 highlights

  • Website Analyzer: stronger multi-signal traffic estimation, automatic Worker retries, cached successful reports and clearer temporary-failure handling.
  • AI Caption Generator: generate from typed context or a public URL, with platform-aware ideas and live web-trend context where available.
  • AI Hashtag Generator: generate from text or a public URL, with separate relevant tags and trend-derived tags.
  • Button system: common action buttons now follow a more consistent DoKitly visual style.
  • Creator & Student cleanup: low-value and duplicate utilities were removed to keep categories focused.
  • About & FAQ: clearer public-facing copy and more useful answers about privacy, live data, accounts and estimates.

Still improving

DoKitly continues to label live data, estimates and external-source features clearly instead of presenting uncertain information as exact.
