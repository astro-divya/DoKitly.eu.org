Cookie Policy

What this policy covers

DoKitly uses browser storage for some preferences and tool workflows and includes Google Analytics and Google AdSense integration. Cookies and similar technologies may also be set by those services when their scripts are active. The exact technologies depend on your browser, location, configuration and whether advertising is enabled.

Essential and local browser storage

Some features store user preferences, shortcut choices or temporary generated results in localStorage or other browser storage. This is not the same as uploading your working document to our server. You can clear DoKitly site data in your browser settings; doing so may remove saved preferences or temporary downloads.

Analytics and advertising

Google Analytics helps us understand aggregate use of pages and actions. Google AdSense, when approved and enabled, may use cookies or similar identifiers to deliver, measure or personalize advertising as permitted. External script providers can receive ordinary network request information such as IP address and browser details.

Your choices and regional consent

Where consent is legally required, supported Google consent messaging must be configured and tested before optional advertising or analytics storage is enabled. A policy page alone is not a consent management platform and does not grant consent. Availability of consent controls depends on the advertising and analytics configuration deployed.

How to manage storage

You can remove or block cookies through browser settings. Google advertising preferences are available through Google My Ad Center. Blocking optional storage can change ad relevance or analytics measurement but should not prevent access to DoKitly core tools.
