About this tool

DoKitly's online typing test measures speed and accuracy while keeping the passage compact and the typing area visible. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty, select a timed duration and use Normal, Strict or God Mode. The passages use educational topics such as science, history, nature and space instead of repetitive filler. Completed words remain visible, Space locks the current word, and mistakes continue to affect the statistics even when a correction is allowed. Use repeated tests under the same settings when you want to compare progress over time.

How the Typing Test Works

Choose the duration, difficulty and mode, then start typing the highlighted reference passage. Space finalizes the current word so you cannot backspace into an earlier word. The passage advances smoothly as lines are completed, while the typing stream keeps your locked text visible. The timer ends the test at zero, and additional text is available for unusually fast typists so the passage does not end early.

What Are WPM and CPM?

Words per minute, or WPM, is a conventional typing-speed measure based on groups of five typed characters rather than the literal number of dictionary words. Characters per minute, or CPM, reports character throughput more directly. Both are useful, but a high speed is only meaningful when accuracy remains strong.

How Accuracy and Errors Are Calculated

Wrong keystrokes are counted as errors even when the current mode allows you to correct the word before pressing Space. This prevents repeated corrections from artificially restoring perfect accuracy. The upper reference passage can preserve an error-history mark after the word is locked, while the lower typed stream shows the final text you actually committed.

Normal vs Strict vs God Mode

Normal and Strict both protect completed words after Space. They are designed for users who want different levels of discipline while still seeing error history in the reference passage. God Mode removes Backspace entirely, so every printable keystroke is final until the word is locked. Because God Mode cannot correct a typed character, it is best used after you are comfortable with the keyboard.

Easy, Medium and Hard Tests

Difficulty changes language complexity rather than making topics boring. Easy passages use common vocabulary and shorter sentences. Medium passages introduce more varied punctuation, dates, numbers and keyboard-accessible brackets or symbols. Hard passages use more complex sentence structure and denser educational material while avoiding obscure Unicode characters that are difficult to enter on a standard English keyboard.

How to Improve Typing Speed and Accuracy

Prioritize rhythm and accuracy before chasing WPM. Keep your hands relaxed, use the correct fingers where possible and look at the reference text rather than the keyboard. Short daily practice is usually more useful than one long session. If errors rise sharply at higher speed, slow down until your accuracy becomes consistent, then increase pace gradually.

Using Your Results

A single WPM score is less useful than a pattern across several tests. Compare speed, accuracy and error count under the same duration and difficulty, then increase difficulty only when the current level feels controlled. If Hard mode causes a sudden accuracy drop, practice Medium passages with punctuation and numbers before trying again. Use God Mode occasionally as a discipline drill rather than as the only way you practice, because real typing also requires controlled correction.

Why Passage Variety Matters

Typing speed can be misleading when you repeatedly practice the same short paragraph because familiarity reduces the reading effort. DoKitly rotates educational material across science, history, nature and other topics so the test remains closer to real reading-and-typing work. Medium and Hard levels also introduce ordinary keyboard punctuation and numbers so the challenge comes from varied text rather than obscure characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the typing test free?

Yes. You can run timed typing tests without creating an account.

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute and is commonly calculated from groups of five typed characters.

What is CPM?

CPM means characters per minute and shows how many characters you type over time.

Does correcting a mistake remove it from accuracy?

No. A wrong keystroke remains part of the error history even when you correct the current word.

What is God Mode?

God Mode disables Backspace, so every keystroke is final until you press Space.

Are Hard passages full of unusual symbols?

No. Hard passages use more difficult language and normal keyboard-accessible punctuation, numbers and symbols rather than obscure Unicode characters.
