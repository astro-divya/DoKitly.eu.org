How ratios work

A ratio compares two or more quantities. To simplify a ratio, divide every part by their greatest common divisor. For example, 12:18 simplifies to 2:3 because both values can be divided by 6.

Ratio examples

Scale 2:3 to 10: multiplying both parts by 5 gives 10:15. Simplify 24:36: divide both parts by 12 to get 2:3. Keeping the same multiplier on every part preserves the proportion.
