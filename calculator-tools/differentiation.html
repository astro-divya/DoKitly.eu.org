Differentiation Calculator

Find symbolic first and higher-order derivatives for common algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic expressions.

Enter a function and choose the derivative order.

Differentiate functions in the browser

The calculator can handle many polynomial, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic expressions. Select the variable and derivative order before solving.

For multivariable expressions, choose the variable you want to differentiate with respect to. The returned expression is symbolic rather than a pre-rounded decimal.
