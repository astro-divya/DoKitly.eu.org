Equation Solver

Solve algebraic equations and simultaneous systems with symbolic results where possible.

Enter an equation. Use one equation per line for a system.

Supported equation input

Use explicit multiplication such as 2*x and standard functions such as sin(x), sqrt(x) and log(x). You can enter multiple equations on separate lines and list the variables as x,y.

Polynomial and many common functional equations can be solved symbolically or numerically by the symbolic engine. Extremely complex or under-specified systems may not produce a closed-form solution.
