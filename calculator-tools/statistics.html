Statistics Calculator

Calculate mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation, quartiles, correlation and linear regression.

Get descriptive statistics and optional two-variable correlation.

Descriptive statistics and correlation

Paste numbers separated by plus signs, commas, spaces or new lines. The result includes count, mean, median, mode, population and sample variance, standard deviation, and quartiles.

For paired X and Y data, enter the same number of values in both boxes to estimate Pearson correlation and a simple linear regression line.
