Disclaimer

Important limits and responsible-use information for DoKitly.

General information

DoKitly provides utility, calculation, study and content-assistance tools. Results should be checked when they are used for legal, financial, medical, academic submission, procurement or other high-stakes decisions.

External data

Some tools depend on public websites, APIs or serverless services. Availability and accuracy can change, and DoKitly does not represent estimated or unavailable data as verified live data.

Competitive-exam practice

Exam-pattern practice is for preparation. A question is described as a previous-year question only when its source is verified. Exam rules, syllabus and marking schemes can change, so official notices remain the authoritative source.

File tools

Keep an original copy before modifying important documents. Browser and device limitations can affect large files or advanced formats.
