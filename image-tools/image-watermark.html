Add & Remove Watermark from Images

Watermark Studio with live preview, mask refinement and full-resolution export.

Add or remove a watermark without uploading your image

DoKitly Watermark Studio combines two workflows on one page. Add a text watermark with custom size, color, opacity, rotation and placement, or clean a watermark from an image you own or are authorized to edit. The editor keeps a live preview visible while you work and prepares the final result at the uploaded image resolution.

Live removal preview

Paint or select the watermark, refine the mask edges, and compare the original with the cleaned preview before exporting.

Auto Detect + manual refine

Auto Detect suggests likely overlay-like edges. Brush, rectangle, erase-mask, expand and feather controls let you correct the selection instead of trusting a false automatic guess.

Private browser processing

Image processing stays in your browser. DoKitly analytics does not receive your image, filename, mask or edited pixels.

How to remove a watermark from an image

  1. Choose an image and open Remove Watermark.
  2. Use Auto Detect for a starting suggestion, or paint/select the watermark manually.
  3. Use Erase Mask, Expand Mask and Feather Edge to keep the selection tight around the unwanted overlay.
  4. Review Cleaned Result, Mask Overlay and Original views. Use the Before / After slider for comparison.
  5. Select Process result to rebuild the result at full resolution, inspect it, then download.

Watermark removal is reconstruction, not recovery of hidden original pixels. Simple backgrounds usually reconstruct more naturally than detailed faces, text, hair or complex textures. Always inspect the final image.

Add a watermark to an image

Use Add Watermark to enter your text, choose color and opacity, rotate it and select a preset position. You can also drag directly on the processed preview for custom placement. This is useful for branding, drafts, proofs and ownership labels.

Watermark remover FAQ

Can I remove a watermark automatically?

Auto Detect can suggest likely text or overlay edges, but no browser tool can reliably identify every flattened watermark. Refine the suggested mask with the brush or rectangle before processing.

Does DoKitly upload my image?

The Watermark Studio processing in this tool runs in your browser. Your image content is not sent to DoKitly analytics.

Can I see the result before downloading?

Yes. Remove mode provides live cleaned, mask-overlay and original previews plus a before-and-after comparison control.

Will the downloaded image use the original resolution?

Yes. The editing preview is optimized for responsiveness, while Process result reconstructs and exports at the uploaded image resolution.

Can I remove any watermark?

Use removal only on images you own or have permission to edit. DoKitly does not grant rights to remove ownership, copyright or licensing marks from third-party content.

Responsible use: removal is intended for your own images or content you have permission to modify. Copyright, licensing and ownership rights still apply.
