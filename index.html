Free online tools for everyday tasks

DoKitly brings practical PDF, image, text, developer, calculator and conversion utilities together in one place. Open a tool, follow its steps and download or copy the result where supported. There is no mandatory signup for the free tools.

For documents, explore PDF to Word, PDF editing, merging, splitting and compression. For images, try resizing, format conversion, compression and background removal. Text and developer tools help with formatting, comparing, validating and transforming everyday data.

Choose a dedicated tool page for instructions, supported formats and limitations. Results depend on the input: scanned PDFs may need OCR, complex documents may require formatting review, and image editing quality varies with the source image.

Many utilities process files locally in your browser. Some features use a live service or external library; check the individual tool interface and the Privacy Policy for the relevant data-handling details. Do not upload confidential material to a feature unless its processing method is clear.

Browse by category to find related workflows instead of repeating manual steps. DoKitly is independently maintained, and feedback helps prioritize fixes and useful additions.
